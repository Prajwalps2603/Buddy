    Open Buddy-1.1.0.apk from your notifications or Downloads.

Good questions

Before you install

I installed an older Buddy. How do I update?

From version 1.1.0, Buddy is signed with its own permanent key. If you installed version 1.0.0, open Buddy and tap Profile → Data & Backup → Sync Now first, then uninstall the old app and install this one. Sign in with the same Google account and everything comes back from your Drive. Future updates install over the top.

Is Buddy really free?

Yes. Buddy is free to use and shows no ads. It has no servers to pay for, because your data lives in your own Google account.

Why isn’t it on the Play Store? Is the download safe?

Buddy is shared directly as an Android app file for now. Android shows a warning for any app installed from outside the Play Store. To be sure your copy hasn’t been changed, compare its SHA-256 with the one under On a computer?

Where is my data stored?

In a folder called Buddy in your own Google Drive, encrypted on your phone with AES-256 before it’s uploaded. Buddy can only open the files it created, never the rest of your Drive. How Drive is used →

What if I forget my passkey?

Use the recovery code you wrote down when you set up your vault. If you lose both, nobody can unlock your files, including the developer. That’s what keeps them private.

Does Buddy read my SMS or email?

Only if you turn on Bank alerts import (SMS and/or Gmail), which is off by default. Buddy then reads only UPI alerts from your bank, on your phone, and saves only the transaction, never the message. Read the details →

Can I use Buddy on more than one phone?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google account and enter your passkey once on the new phone. Buddy keeps your devices in step and works offline in between.
