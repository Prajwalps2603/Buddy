Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Buddy (“the app”), developed by Prajwal Nair, does with your information: what it accesses, where it is stored, how it is synced, updated and protected, and how you stay in control. Buddy has no servers of its own: your data stays in your own Google account and on your phone.

1Summary

Key point

Your data lives in your own Google Drive and Google Sheets and on your phone. The developer cannot see, access or sell it.

  • No Buddy account and no Buddy servers. You sign in with Google; your records are stored in your Google account.
  • Limited Google access: Buddy can only open files it creates or that you add through it — never the rest of your Drive, your contacts or password. Gmail is read only if you turn on bank-email import, and then only for bank UPI alerts.
  • Everything encrypted: your records in Buddy_Data and your documents, receipts, backups and reports are encrypted on your phone with AES-256 before upload, and can only be read in Buddy with your passkey.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing of your data.
  • You are in control: export, edit, delete, disconnect or delete your account at any time.

2Information Buddy uses

Key point

Buddy only uses what you enter or choose to add, plus your basic Google profile to show who is signed in.

Buddy does not collect your location, contacts, call logs, messages, browsing history or advertising ID.

  • Google profile (from Google Sign-In): your name, email address and profile photo.
  • Financial records you enter: expenses, income, accounts and transfers, loans, budgets, savings goals, recurring payments, Pocket Money, salary and payment reminders.
  • Organiser data you enter: calendar entries, reminders, tasks, events, weekend work logs, notes and document details (name, category, issue and expiry dates).
  • Profile details you choose to give: name, whether you are a student, age, salary or pocket money, savings target, currency and timezone.
  • Files you add: documents and receipt photos you upload or scan with the camera.
  • Device preferences: theme, notification and security settings, stored only on your phone.
  • Bank UPI alerts (optional, off by default): if you turn on Bank email import, Buddy reads your Gmail read-only to find emails from your bank about UPI payments, and saves only the amount, date, time, person or merchant, UPI reference and bank as an expense or income.

3How Google Drive is used

Key point

Buddy creates one “Buddy” folder in your Drive and works only inside it, using Google’s most limited Drive permission (drive.file).

When you connect Google, Buddy asks for one permission: “See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app.” With this permission Buddy can open only the files and folders it created or that you added through it. It cannot list, read or change any other file in your Drive.

Inside your Drive, Buddy keeps:

  • Buddy/Documents/<category> — your uploaded documents (encrypted).
  • Buddy/Receipts — receipt photos attached to expenses (encrypted).
  • Buddy/Finance/Buddy_Data — the spreadsheet holding your records (see the next section).
  • Buddy/Reports — monthly reports you export (encrypted).
  • Buddy/Backup — backups you or Buddy create (encrypted).
  • Buddy/.buddy-vault.json — your locked encryption key (it can only be unlocked with your passkey or recovery code).

4How Google Sheets is used

Key point

Your records are stored as rows in a spreadsheet called Buddy_Data in your own Google Drive. The contents of every row are encrypted, so the sheet can only be read inside Buddy, with your passkey.

Buddy_Data has one tab per kind of record (for example Expenses, Income, Budgets, Calendar, Notes, Documents). Each row has a unique ID and an “UpdatedAt” time, which Buddy uses to keep your devices in step. Deleted records are marked as deleted rather than erased immediately, so a sync can never lose data.

What stays readable: only each row’s random ID, its “UpdatedAt” time and whether it is deleted — the minimum Buddy needs to sync. Everything else (amounts, names, notes, categories, dates, document names and the rest) is sealed with AES-256-GCM into a single “EncryptedData” column. The only settings left readable are technical ones: whether encryption is on and the IDs of Buddy’s own folders.

Switching on encryption: the first time a phone with your passkey syncs, Buddy creates a new, encrypted Buddy_Data and moves the old readable sheet to your Drive trash (renamed “Buddy_Data (old, unencrypted)”), so no readable copy is left in the sheet’s version history. Google empties the trash after 30 days; you can empty it sooner.

Because the rows are encrypted, editing them by hand in Google Sheets is not possible — use Buddy, or export your data as CSV or a PDF report. Buddy writes values as plain text (never as formulas), so text you type can’t become a spreadsheet formula.

5How syncing and updates work

Key point

Buddy works offline first: everything is saved on your phone immediately and uploaded to your Google account when you’re online.

  • On your phone: a private local database keeps a copy of your records so the app works without internet. Changes made offline wait in a queue.
  • Sync: when online, Buddy uploads waiting changes to Buddy_Data and downloads changes made on your other devices. It only downloads when the spreadsheet has actually changed.
  • Encrypted sync: rows are encrypted on your phone before upload and decrypted on your phone after download. On a new phone, Buddy asks for your passkey once; until then nothing is uploaded from that phone, so readable data can never reach the sheet.
  • Conflicts: if the same record changed on two devices, the most recent change wins.
  • Retries: if a sync fails (no internet, Google busy), Buddy retries automatically, waiting a little longer each time. Each record has a unique ID, so retries never create duplicates.
  • Phone calendar (optional): if you allow it, calendar entries are also added to your phone’s calendar. Buddy only changes the events it created.
  • App updates are a new download from this website, installed over the old version. Your data is not stored inside the app, so updating, reinstalling or changing phones never deletes it. Sign in with the same Google account to continue.

6Encryption and the documents vault

Key point

Your records, documents, receipts, backups and reports are encrypted on your phone before upload. In Google Drive and Sheets they are unreadable, and only Buddy can open them, with your passkey. Files are stored as .buddy files.

  • Strong encryption: files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, which also detects any tampering. Each file uses a fresh random value.
  • Your own passkey is never stored or sent anywhere. (Until you change it, the default name-based passkey is kept on your phone so Buddy can remind you what it is.) Buddy turns it into a key with scrypt, a deliberately slow method that makes guessing very costly. That key unlocks your file key.
  • Default passkey: when the vault is first set up, the passkey is your name without spaces. Buddy asks you to set your own passkey straight away, because a name is easy to guess.
  • Recovery code: a one-time code you write down. It is the only way back in if you forget your passkey — nobody, including the developer, can recover your files without it.
  • Unlocking: with your passkey or, if you turn it on, your fingerprint or face (your biometric data never leaves your phone). The vault locks again automatically after the app has been in the background.
  • Fingerprint for Documents: by default Buddy also asks for your fingerprint, face or phone screen lock each time you open Documents or the vault settings. You can turn this off in Profile → App Lock.
  • Opening files: a decrypted copy is kept briefly in the app’s private storage while you view it, and deleted when the vault locks.
  • Background sync and automatic backups: after you enter your passkey once on a phone, a copy of the file key is kept in that phone’s secure keystore (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain, usable only on that device), so syncing and scheduled backups can stay encrypted without asking every time. Documents still need the vault unlocked. Signing out, deactivating or deleting your account removes the key from the phone.
  • Backups waiting for your passkey: if a backup is due on a phone that doesn’t have the key yet, Buddy asks for your passkey and then finishes the backup. A backup is never saved unencrypted while the vault is on.

7Security

Key point

Your data is protected in transit, in storage and on your phone.

  • In transit: all communication with Google uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Google sign-in: handled by Google’s official sign-in on your phone. Buddy never sees or stores your Google password. Access tokens are kept only in memory, never written to files or the spreadsheet, and never logged.
  • App lock: an optional PIN (stored only as a salted hash in your phone’s secure keystore) and fingerprint or face unlock, with automatic locking.
  • On your phone: Buddy’s data is stored in its private app storage, which other apps can’t read.
  • Exports: CSV exports protect against spreadsheet formula injection.

8Phone permissions

Buddy asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and works without it if you say no.

  • Camera: to scan receipts and documents.
  • Photos: to attach an existing photo as a receipt.
  • Calendar: to add your entries to your phone’s calendar (optional).
  • Notifications: for reminders, payment due dates and salary reminders. They are scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server.
  • Fingerprint / face: to unlock the app or your documents. Biometric data stays with your phone’s operating system.
  • Home-screen widget (optional): if you add it, it shows today’s safe-to-spend or spending on your home screen. With “hide amounts” on in Buddy, the widget hides amounts too.
  • Internet: to sync with your Google account.

9Sharing and selling

Key point

We do not sell, rent or share your data, and Buddy shows no advertising.

  • Your data goes only to your own Google account (Google Drive and Google Sheets), which processes it under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Files leave Buddy only when you choose to share them (for example a report or a document) using your phone’s share menu.
  • Emergency kit: if you create one, it is locked on your phone with a code only you give out, and goes only where you send it. Buddy keeps no copy.
  • Pay now and request money: these open your own UPI app (or WhatsApp) with the details filled in. Buddy never sees your UPI PIN and never moves money.
  • Crash reports (only in builds where they are switched on): if the app crashes, a report with the error and basic device details (model, system version) may be sent to help fix bugs. It never includes your name, email, records, amounts, notes or files.

10Bank email import (Gmail, optional)

Key point

Off unless you turn it on. Buddy then uses only UPI alerts sent from your bank’s own email address, on your phone, with read-only access, and never stores or shares your emails.

  • Permission: when you turn it on, Google asks you to allow read-only Gmail access (“gmail.readonly”). Buddy never asks for it at sign-in.
  • What is searched: received messages mentioning UPI or a UPI ID together with words like “debited” or “credited”, from the last 30 days and then only new mail (never your sent mail or drafts). Matching messages are opened on your phone only to check them; anything not sent from a bank’s own email address, and OTPs, payment requests, failed and future (mandate) debits, is ignored and discarded.
  • What is kept: only the transaction (amount, date, time, person or merchant, UPI reference, bank and last digits of the account), saved as an expense (debited) or income (credited) in your encrypted Buddy_Data. The email itself is never saved, uploaded or shown to anyone.
  • Read-only: Buddy cannot send, delete, label or change any email.
  • Your control: imported entries can be edited or deleted like any other. Turn the import off in Profile → Bank alerts import, and remove Gmail access completely in your Google Account → Security → Third-party access.

11Google API Services User Data Policy

Buddy’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide Buddy’s features to you. It is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not read by people.

12Keeping and deleting your data

Key point

Your data stays in your Google account until you delete it. You can remove it from your phone or delete it completely at any time.

  • Log out (Profile): removes Buddy’s data from this phone. Your Google data stays.
  • Deactivate account (Profile → Google Account & Devices → Disconnect Google, or Profile → Data & Backup): removes Buddy’s access to your Google account and clears this phone. Your data stays in Drive; sign in again to reactivate.
  • Delete account (Profile → Account → Delete Account): moves your whole Buddy folder to Google Drive trash (Google deletes it permanently after 30 days, or immediately if you empty the trash), removes phone-calendar events Buddy created, revokes access and clears this phone.
  • Revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

13Your rights and control

  • See and correct: view and edit all your records in Buddy. (Buddy_Data is encrypted, so it can’t be edited by hand.)
  • Export: download your expenses and income as CSV, or monthly PDF and CSV reports.
  • Delete: delete individual records, or your whole account as described above.
  • Withdraw consent: disconnect Google at any time; Buddy then stops accessing your data.

14Children and students

You must meet Google’s minimum age to use a Google account in your country. If you are under 18, use Buddy only with a parent’s or guardian’s permission. Student details such as age and pocket money are stored only in your own Google account.

15Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, Buddy will show it to you again and ask you to accept it before continuing. The date at the top shows the latest version.
