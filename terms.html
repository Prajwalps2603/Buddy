1Using Buddy

Buddy is a personal organiser for money, documents, calendar and notes, developed by Prajwal Nair. By using it you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2Your Google account

Key point

Buddy works through your own Google account and stores your data there.

Your use of Google services is also covered by Google’s Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. You authorise Buddy to create and manage its own files in your Google Drive, and you can withdraw this authorisation at any time.

3Your data and responsibilities

  • You own everything you put into Buddy.
  • You are responsible for the accuracy of what you record, and for keeping your phone, your Google account and your passkey and recovery code safe.
  • If you lose both your passkey and your recovery code, your encrypted records and files cannot be recovered by anyone, including the developer.
  • Do not use Buddy for anything unlawful, or to store other people’s personal information without their permission.

4Not financial advice

Balances, budgets, insights, salary dates and projections are calculated from what you enter. They are for your own planning and are not financial, tax or legal advice. Weekend work records are personal notes; Buddy does not decide whether extra pay is owed.

5Availability

Buddy depends on your phone and on Google’s services. We aim to keep it working well but cannot promise it will always be available or error-free. Keep backups of anything important (Profile → Data & Backup).

Buddy is provided “as is”. To the extent the law allows, the developer is not liable for any loss of data, money or opportunity arising from use of the app. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under consumer law that cannot be limited.

7Stopping use

You can stop using Buddy at any time: log out, deactivate, or delete your account from Profile → Data & Backup.
